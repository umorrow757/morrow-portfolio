How we work together

  1. Listen

    We start by hearing how your team works today.

  2. Plan

    We agree on what to change first, in plain words.

  3. Build

    We set it up with your team, one piece at a time.

  4. Check

    We check that it works for people, then adjust.

The Dance Floor

Every decision is checked in order: Safe (legal included) first, then Thoughtful, Efficient and Effective. Three pretend decisions, checked gate by gate.

Open a night shift for nap supervisors
  1. SafePass

    Two keepers on every night shift, and the fire code checked first.

  2. ThoughtfulPass

    Keepers pick their nights. Nobody is moved without being asked.

  3. EfficientFix

    Start with two nights a week, then add more.

  4. EffectivePass

    Late pick-ups are covered, and keepers keep their weekends.

Approved with one fix. This idea can dance on our dance floor.
