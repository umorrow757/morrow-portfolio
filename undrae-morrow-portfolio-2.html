The Vision

Stop sending documents.
Start shipping experiences.

My vision is a new-age, innovative way to communicate. The old forms — documents, PDFs, and spreadsheets — ask people to read information and do the work of understanding it themselves. I replace them with interactive websites and web apps, content-style videos, and interactive dashboards and graphs that people can explore, tap, and actually use. Information shouldn't sit still. It should move, respond, and meet people where they are.

The old form

  • A PDF budget emailed around
  • A spreadsheet nobody opens twice
  • A 20-page report before a decision
  • A résumé that flattens a person
  • Training decks people click through
→

The new form — what I build

  • A live dashboard with charts that update the story
  • A web app the whole household or team lives in
  • A short decision video with tappable choices
  • An interactive candidate page with a submit video
  • A game or simulation people learn by playing
