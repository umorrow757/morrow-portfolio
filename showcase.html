SAMPLE / MOCK DATA

Solution Command Center

What it replaced
Checking the ATS, the job board inbox, email and chat by hand every morning to build the day's call list.
How it works
One screen for a recruiter's whole book of business: who to contact today, every active candidate, every open req and a change log, refreshed daily. The sample below keeps the real structure and swaps in a pretend dragon daycare, so every name, number and pay rate is invented.
How it is fed
Fed by a sourcing tracker worksheet he built with live data connections to the team's recruiting systems, and by his own outreach templates for text, email, and messaging.
What he built
Not just the front end. The data plumbing is his too: the sourcing tracker worksheet, its live data connections to the team's recruiting systems (built on OData, an open data standard), and the outreach templates behind the Message builder.
The daily loop
Each workday starts from a fresh file. Work the queue top-down, mark what went out, and your own corrections ride over the next refresh.
